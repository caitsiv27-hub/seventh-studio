Our Story

A Home Away From
Home.

Amber House Guest Lodge has been welcoming South African families for over 15 years. Nestled at the foot of the Drakensberg escarpment, we offer a peaceful retreat where children roam freely, braai fires burn at sunset, and the only alarm clock is birdsong.

Whether you're here for a long weekend or a full week, our warm and experienced team will make sure you leave feeling rested, recharged, and already planning your next visit.

Fully equipped self-catering kitchens in all units
Secure parking and 24-hour security
Swimming pool, braai facilities, and children's play area
Hiking trails accessible directly from the lodge
15 minutes from Cathedral Peak and Champagne Valley
